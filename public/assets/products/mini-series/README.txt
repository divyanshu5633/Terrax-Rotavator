Place product photos for the Mini Series here.

Filename convention:
  hero.webp       — Main series hero image
  gallery-1.webp  — Gallery image 1
  gallery-2.webp  — Gallery image 2
  gallery-3.webp  — Gallery image 3

Photo requirements:
  - Minimum 1200px wide
  - WebP format preferred (JPEG acceptable)
  - Product on clean background or field context
  - No watermarks

Update series.ts imagePath and galleryPaths when photos are added.
